# Code of conduct

GUPPI is a small fan project. Everyone taking part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent. Most mistakes are honest ones, and a bad bug report is still a bug report.
- Keep it on topic and keep it civil. No harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, or sharing of anyone's private information.
- Remember that the game belongs to its authors. Do not use the project to attack other players or the game's servers.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these expectations, and may block repeat offenders. If something happens that you think the maintainers should know about, report it privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/Izzo2thePoint/GUPPI/security/advisories/new) or by messaging the maintainer on GitHub.
